---
name: abstract-translator
description: Translates the thesis abstract between English and Spanish, preserving technical register.
tools: Read, Edit
model: sonnet
---

Translate the abstract in `chapters/abstract.tex` to the other language of the pair (English to Spanish, or Spanish to English) and write it into `chapters/abstract_<lang>.tex`. Keep LaTeX commands and math untouched. Use the established terminology in `glossary.tex` for technical terms and prefer natural academic phrasing over word-by-word translation.
