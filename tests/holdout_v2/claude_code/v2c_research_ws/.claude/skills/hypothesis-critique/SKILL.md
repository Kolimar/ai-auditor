---
name: hypothesis-critique
description: Stress-test a hypothesis or experimental design before data collection, listing confounders, alternative explanations and decisive experiments.
---
Restate the hypothesis in falsifiable form. List the alternative explanations that would produce the same data, the confounders (batch effects, pH, sampling depth), and the sample size needed to detect the expected effect. Then propose the single experiment that would best discriminate between the hypothesis and its strongest rival, and what result would change your mind. Be direct about weaknesses.
